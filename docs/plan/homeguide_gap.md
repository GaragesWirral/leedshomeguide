# HomeGuide vs Leeds Home Guide: what we are missing (11 Nov 2026)

Research only. Nothing on leedshomeguide.co.uk was changed or deployed.

How this was read: homeguide.com blocks plain fetches (Cloudflare challenge, 403), so its pages were read through a public page reader on 11 Nov 2026: homepage, /near-me (full service index), /costs, /plumbers, /tx/austin/plumbers (city page), /costs/plumber-cost (cost guide), a pro profile, /pro (the "for pros" page), /safety, /asbestos-removal. Leeds Home Guide was crawled from its live sitemap (15 pages). Leeds search demand is from our own Google Keyword Planner export of 8 Nov 2026 (files/seo/leeds_keywords_kp_2026-11-08.csv): KP gives ranges only, and "—" or "?" means KP returned nothing.

## Top 10 actions (ranked)

1. **Add the missing trades.** HomeGuide indexes 1,059 service types; we have 4 trades. Each new trade page is a new Leeds search we can rank for and 3 more firms to send the badge to. Start with the trades that have the most Leeds searches: locksmiths and skip hire (KP 1K–10K a month in Leeds each), then painters and decorators, removals, builders, plasterers, kitchen fitters, bathroom fitters, landscapers, driveways, fencing, loft conversions, gutter cleaning, pest control, cleaners (KP 100–1K each). Full list below.
2. **A page for each firm we list.** HomeGuide gives every pro a profile page (about, specialties, photos, reviews, services, areas, related links). For us that means one page per firm (what they do, areas covered, the checks we made, contact, logo). It gives the badge a page to link to ("see our listing") and trades want a page about themselves. It also ranks for firm-name searches.
3. **"Cost in Leeds 2026" guides, one per trade.** HomeGuide has about 1,557 cost guide links on /costs, and every trade page links to 15–20 of them. Same format: headline range, a cost table, a price list by job, what changes the price, call-out fees, FAQs, hiring tips, "updated" date, and a link back to the Leeds firms. UK demand from KP: new boiler cost, EICR cost, roof replacement cost, loft conversion cost, extension cost are 1K–10K UK searches a month each (10–100 in Leeds). Every figure must come from a named UK source (see "Sources for cost guides").
4. **Group the trades the way HomeGuide does.** Their homepage sorts trades into groups (Popular, Remodeling, Repair and maintenance, Improvement, Landscaping, Construction, Cleaning), each with "See all". Our /trades/ page is a flat list of 4 plus a "Coming soon" line. Once we have 15+ trades: groups like Repairs, Heating and energy, Home improvements, Outdoor and garden, Cleaning and moving.
5. **A linking block on every trade page.** Every HomeGuide city page ends with "Nearby [trade] in and around [city]", "[city] [trade] by service" and "Related cost guides". Ours have one "Find a local Leeds trade" link set at the end of the guides only. Add: related trades, the matching cost guides, and (later) areas. This is how HomeGuide spreads strength across 1,000+ pages, and it is how the guide will pass strength on to YAS and the rank-and-rent pages.
6. **Job pages under the big trades.** HomeGuide splits trades into jobs ("Austin plumbers by service": water heaters, pipe repair, etc.). The Leeds versions with demand: emergency plumber Leeds (KP 100–1K in Leeds, top bid £9–£29, the most expensive click in our list), boiler installation Leeds (100–1K UK), roof repair Leeds (100–1K UK), resin driveway Leeds (100–1K UK, bid up to £20.61), asbestos survey Leeds (100–1K UK).
7. **A working listing request form and a working email.** HomeGuide has a full "Join as a pro" page (/pro: free to join, how leads work, FAQs, sign-up form). Ours says "A listing request form is coming soon" and "Form coming soon", and the email shown, hello@leedshomeguide.co.uk, only works once today's 1:30 PM inbox setup lands. Before badge emails go out, a trade who replies needs somewhere to go: a "For trades" page with the free listing, the badge, what we check, and a form.
8. **Area pages (Headingley, Horsforth, Chapel Allerton, Roundhay, Kirkstall first).** HomeGuide has a page for every city and every trade in that city, plus "Top cities" and "all 50 states" lists. The Leeds equivalent is areas. The place names get a lot of searches (KP, Leeds searches a month: Headingley 10K–100K; Chapel Allerton, Horsforth, Roundhay and Kirkstall 1K–10K each), but those people are not always looking for a trade, and "plumber Horsforth" style searches were not in our KP pull. With 3 firms per trade, area × trade pages would be near copies, which Google treats as thin. So: area hub pages first, with real local content (housing types, conservation areas, which firms cover it), and area × trade pages only where we have firms based there.
9. **Ratings and reviews, done honestly.** HomeGuide shows a star rating, review count, review quotes and "hires on HomeGuide" on every card. We show none (on purpose). The honest version, already in the README plan: the firm's Google rating and review count with the date we read it and a link to the reviews. No reviews we have not read, no made-up quotes.
10. **A "get quotes" button and a hiring tips hub.** HomeGuide's whole model is "Request a quote" on every card and "Get free estimates" on every cost guide, plus a /safety "Tips for hiring" page. We could add a simple "Get quotes from Leeds firms" form that passes jobs to listed firms (this is what makes trades want a listing, and it is what can be charged for later), and turn the homepage's "How to find a good tradesperson" section into its own guide page. The form needs Joseph's decision (see below).

Smaller ones: no social share image (og:image is missing on every page), only 2 guides live, and the homepage "New boiler: Coming soon" card points at a guide that does not exist yet.

## 5. Decisions for Joseph
1. **Firms per trade:** stay at 3 (his rule) or go to 6–10 like HomeGuide's "Top 10"? More firms = more badge emails and fuller pages.
2. **Which trades first.** Suggested: locksmiths, painters and decorators, builders, plasterers, landscapers, driveways, then kitchen/bathroom fitters and removals.
3. **Badge emails only reach Ltd/LLP firms** (UK cold email rules), so lists should lean on Ltd firms if the point is backlinks. Sole traders can still be listed; they just cannot be cold emailed.
4. **Google ratings on cards:** show them with the date read, or keep cards rating-free?
5. **Quote request form:** yes or no. It is what makes trades care most and what could be charged for later, but it means handling homeowners' details (privacy page, who gets each lead).
6. **Firm profile pages and photos:** ask firms for photos and a short description when we send the badge?
7. **Linking to his own sites:** YAS is already listed as a normal firm. Rank-and-rent pages are not real firms with staff, so they should not be listed as firms; linking to them from guide text is OK only where it is genuinely useful. Worth agreeing before the trade pages go up.
8. **Area pages:** which areas, and whether to wait until we have firms based there.
