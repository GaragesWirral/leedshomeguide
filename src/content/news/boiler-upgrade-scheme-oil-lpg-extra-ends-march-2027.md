The Boiler Upgrade Scheme helps homeowners in England and Wales replace a fossil fuel boiler with a low carbon heating system. The grant is paid to the installer and taken off your bill. The government updated the scheme page on 30 October 2026.

## How much the grant is

| System | Grant |
|---|---|
| Air source heat pump (air to water) | £7,500 |
| Ground source heat pump | £7,500 |
| Biomass boiler | £5,000 |
| Air to air heat pump | £2,500 |

There is one grant per property.

## The extra £1,500, and when it ends

If your home is heated by oil or LPG and has no mains gas connection, you can get an extra £1,500 towards an air source or ground source heat pump. This top up ends in March 2027.

This mainly helps homes off the gas network, such as some farmhouses and villages on the rural edges of the Leeds district. If that is you and you are thinking about a heat pump, it is worth getting quotes this winter.

## How to get it

You do not apply yourself. Your installer applies for the grant, so choose an MCS certified installer who is signed up to the scheme. The grant can be combined with a low interest loan from the new [Warm Homes Loan Scheme](/news/warm-homes-loan-scheme-launch/).

## Staying with gas?

Gas boilers do not qualify for this grant. If you are replacing a gas boiler like for like, see our [new boiler cost guide](/guides/new-boiler-cost/) or our list of [Leeds plumbers](/trades/plumbers/).
