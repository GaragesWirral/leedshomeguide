The Warm Homes Loan Scheme is now open to households across the UK. It is part of the government's Warm Homes Plan, and it works through high street and specialist lenders rather than through the council or the government directly. The government factsheet was published on 27 October 2026.

## What the loans cover

The scheme gives lenders funding to cut the interest on loans for low carbon home upgrades. The government says rates are up to 5 percentage points lower than a regular loan, some loans are interest free, and some can be repaid over as long as thirty years.

The most you can borrow depends on what you are installing:

| Upgrade | Maximum loan |
|---|---|
| Solar panels | £15,000 |
| Battery storage | £15,000 |
| Air to water heat pump | £20,000 |
| Air to air heat pump | £10,000 |
| Ground source heat pump | £35,000 |
| Biomass boiler | £20,000 |
| Heat network connection | £7,500 |

The government's own example is a £10,000 loan over seven years: at 8% that is about £155 a month, and through the scheme you could save up to £285 a year on repayments.

## How to apply

You apply to a participating lender or through an installer that offers scheme finance, not to the government. Ten lenders signed up first, including Tandem Bank, Secure Trust Bank, thisbank and The Money Co-op, and more are expected. The lender runs its own credit and affordability checks.

The installer must be MCS certified (except for heat network connections). Keep the quote, the finance agreement, the invoice and the MCS certificate.

## Can I combine it with a grant?

Yes, for heat pumps. A Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant can be put towards the same heat pump as the loan. The grant is currently £7,500 for an air source or ground source heat pump in England and Wales.

## What it means if you live in Leeds

If your gas boiler is near the end of its life, it is worth pricing a heat pump with a grant and a scheme loan alongside a like for like boiler swap. Our [new boiler cost guide](/guides/new-boiler-cost/) shows what a replacement gas boiler costs, and solar panels and batteries need a qualified [electrician](/trades/electricians/) for the connection work.
