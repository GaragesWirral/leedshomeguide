Leeds City Council has confirmed that brown bin collections pause for the winter. The final round started on Monday 16 November 2026, and the last scheduled collection day is Saturday 28 November 2026. Collections start again in the spring, and the council has not yet given a restart date.

## When is my last brown bin collection?

It depends on your usual collection day, so check your address on the council's bin day checker. The Leeds Bins app can also send you a reminder the day before.

## What to do with garden waste over winter

The council suggests three options:

- **Take it to a recycling site.** Leeds has eight council household waste recycling centres. They are free to use and open seven days a week, 8am to 4pm.
- **Compost it at home.** Fallen leaves make good mulch or leaf mould if you bag them and leave them to rot down.
- **Keep it until spring.** Store it somewhere dry and put it out once collections restart.

Households on the combined food and garden waste trial in the Wetherby area keep their brown bin collections all winter.

If you are cutting back hedges or clearing a whole garden before spring, a car boot of waste at a time may not be enough.
