The council's fee is only part of what planning permission costs. You will usually also pay for drawings, sometimes for surveys, and possibly for advice before you apply. This guide sets out the fees for Leeds homeowners in 2026 from the Planning Portal, Leeds City Council and Checkatrade.

## How much does a planning application cost?

| Application in England | Fee | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Householder: extension or alteration to a single house | £548 | Planning Portal, from 1 April 2026 |
| Full application: one new house (per house, up to 50) | £610 | Checkatrade, March 2026 |
| Alterations or extensions to two or more homes | £1,083 | Checkatrade, March 2026 |
| Outline application, per 0.1 hectare | £659 | Checkatrade, March 2026 |

Leeds is in England, so these national fees apply. In Wales the householder fee is £585.

## The Planning Portal service charge

Most people apply online through the Planning Portal. It adds a service charge to applications with a fee over £100, in three bands: Band 0 is free, Band 1 is **£99 including VAT** and Band 2 is £149 including VAT. The Planning Portal says most applications are either free of the charge or in Band 1. You can avoid it by applying directly to Leeds City Council.

## Leeds pre application advice

Before applying, you can ask Leeds City Council planners for advice on whether your plans are likely to be approved. The council's pre application fees for homeowners are:

| Advice | Fee including VAT |
|---|---|
| House extensions, outbuildings and alterations | £156 |
| Alterations and extensions to a listed building | £216 |
| Advice on discharging conditions, minor applications | £140 |

Source: Leeds City Council pre application enquiry service. It is worth considering for two storey extensions, homes in conservation areas or anything close to a boundary.

## Drawings and other costs

| Cost | Typical figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Single storey extension application, all in | £1,000 to £2,500 | Checkatrade |
| Two storey extension application, all in | £2,000 to £3,500 | Checkatrade |
| New build application, all in | £3,000 to £5,000 | Checkatrade |
| Planning consultant | about £215 an hour | Checkatrade |
| Approving conditions, householder application | £46 | Checkatrade |

You may also need a measured survey, a tree survey if trees are nearby, or a heritage statement in a conservation area.

## Do you need planning permission?

Many single storey rear extensions, loft conversions and outbuildings fall under permitted development and do not need a planning application, within size limits. Two storey, side and front extensions, and most work to listed buildings or in conservation areas, usually do. If your project is permitted development and you want proof, for example for a future sale, a lawful development certificate costs half the normal fee.

Building regulations approval is separate and is needed for almost every extension, whether or not it needs planning.

## Prior approval, outline and other applications

Some larger single storey rear extensions can go through the prior approval route instead of a full householder application, which has its own lower fee. Outline planning applications are charged per 0.1 hectare of site area, and change of use applications have their own fees. The Planning Portal's fee calculator works out the correct fee for each of the application types under the Town and Country Planning rules, and the local planning authority, Leeds City Council, confirms the fee payable when you submit an application. The Planning Portal also notes a range of exemptions and reductions.

## Types of planning application and their fees

| Type of planning application | What it is for | Fee in England |
|---|---|---|
| Householder application | Extensions and alterations to one house | £548 (Planning Portal) |
| Full planning application | A new home or larger works | £610 per new house (Checkatrade) |
| Outline planning application | Agreeing the principle before full details | £659 per 0.1 hectare (Checkatrade) |
| Lawful development certificate, proposed | Proof that a project is permitted development | half the normal fee |
| Approval of conditions, householder | Discharging conditions after approval | £46 (Checkatrade) |
| Listed building consent | Works to a listed building | no fee |

The application fee is payable when you submit an application, and the local planning authority will not start work on an application until the correct fee is paid. Fees for planning applications are set nationally under the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 regulations, so the planning fee in Leeds is the same as anywhere else in England. Some works are exempt, for example works solely to improve access for a disabled person. If you are unsure, the Planning Portal fee calculator works out the correct fee before you apply.
