Most locksmith calls are one of three things: a lockout, a lock change, or a lock that has stopped working. This guide sets out what each costs in 2026, using two national cost guides, and what pushes the price up.

| When you call | Average hourly rate |
|---|---|

## Locksmith prices in Leeds

Checkatrade publishes average job prices for a handful of UK cities. For Leeds it gives about **£149**, compared with £135 in Sheffield, £140 in Bolton and £163 in London (May 2026). That is an average across all job types, so a simple daytime lock change is often cheaper and a night time lockout dearer.

## What changes the price?

- **Time of day and day of the week.** Nights, weekends and bank holidays cost more.
- **The lock.** High security and anti snap cylinders cost more than basic ones, and MyBuilder puts replacement locks at £70 to £165 depending on type.
- **Damage.** If the lock has to be drilled, you pay for a new one.
- **Travel.** MyBuilder notes travel charges of about £20 to £50 if the locksmith comes a long way, which is a good reason to use a local locksmith.

## Avoiding a rogue locksmith

Locksmiths are not regulated in the UK. Which? warns that some firms advertise at the top of search results, quote a low price on the phone and then charge hundreds more on the doorstep. Agree a total price including VAT and parts before anyone sets off, and check the firm on the [Master Locksmiths Association](https://www.locksmiths.co.uk/locksmith-leeds/) register.
