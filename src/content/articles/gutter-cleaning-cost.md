Blocked gutters overflow, soak walls and can lead to damp inside. Cleaning them is a quick job for a firm with the right kit. This guide sets out what gutter cleaning costs in 2026, using two national cost guides.

## Per metre, hourly and day rates

MyBuilder gives £4 to £12 per metre of guttering. Checkatrade gives £20 to £30 an hour, or £150 to £250 for a day, which suits blocks of flats or long runs of terraces.

## Vacuum systems or ladders

Most gutter cleaners now use a gutter vacuum on a long pole, with a camera so they can show you the gutter before and after. It means most two storey houses can be cleaned from the ground. Ladders are still used for some jobs, and very tall or awkward roofs may need a cherry picker or scaffolding, which is the main reason prices go up.

## What pushes the price up

- **Height.** Three storey houses and steep gardens take longer and need more kit.
- **Length of guttering.** A detached house has far more gutter than a terrace.
- **How blocked it is.** Moss, plants and years of silt take longer to clear.
- **Access.** Conservatories, extensions and narrow passages slow the job down.
- **Repairs.** Leaking joints and broken brackets are extra.

## When to get gutters cleaned

Late autumn, once the leaves have fallen, is the most common time. Homes near trees may need a second clean in spring. If water spills over the gutter in heavy rain, or plants are growing out of it, it is overdue.
