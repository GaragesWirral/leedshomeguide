A new roof is a big job, and prices vary widely with the shape of the roof, the materials and access. This guide uses two national cost guides to show what a roof replacement costs in 2026.

## How much does a roof replacement cost?

| Roof type | Typical UK cost (plus VAT) | Per m² | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hip roof | £9,250 to £18,000 | £145 to £285 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| Gable roof | £7,000 to £16,250 | £110 to £260 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| Flat roof | £2,800 to £7,500 | £45 to £120 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| New roof structure | £6,500 to £20,000+ | | Checkatrade, October 2026 |

Checkatrade's UK average is around **£8,000**, with about £12,000 for a three bedroom semi and £17,000 for a four bedroom detached house.

MyBuilder gives lower figures for simpler roofs: £5,000 to £8,000 for a gable roof, £8,000 to £12,000 for a hipped roof, £2,500 to £6,000 for a flat roof and £3,000 to £5,500 for a lean to.

## Extra costs to check in a roofing quote

| Extra | Typical cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Removing the old roof, if not included | £1,000 to £2,500 | Checkatrade |
| Scaffolding (set up, removal and weekly hire) | £700 to £1,300 | Checkatrade |
| Scaffolding | about £1,125 | MyBuilder |
| Disposal of old materials | £100 to £1,000 | MyBuilder |
| Skip (6 to 8 yards) | about £320 a week | Checkatrade |
| Roofer day rate | £250 to £300 | MyBuilder |

## Roofs in Leeds

Many older Leeds houses have Welsh slate or stone slate roofs, while post war houses often have concrete tiles. Natural slate costs more than concrete tiles but lasts longer. If your house is in a conservation area, check with Leeds City Council before changing the roof material.

## Repair or replace?

A few slipped slates or failed flashing is a repair job. Widespread slipped slates, rotten battens or leaks in several places suggest replacement. See [roof repair in Leeds](/trades/roofers/roof-repair/) for what a roofer checks.

## Getting quotes

1. Get at least two written quotes listing materials, scaffolding, waste and guarantees.
2. Ask whether the guarantee is insurance backed.
3. Check whether new felt, battens and ventilation are included.
