A damp survey finds the type and cause of damp before you spend money on treatment. This guide sets out what a damp survey costs, using MyBuilder's 2026 guide and Checkatrade's older figures, and explains what you should get for your money.

## How much does a damp survey cost?

| Survey or property | Typical UK cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Most damp surveys | £150 to £500 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Basic survey (visual and moisture meter) | £150 to £250 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Comprehensive survey (thermal imaging, borescope) | £200 to £500 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Small flat | £150 to £200 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Terraced house | £180 to £250 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Semi detached house | £200 to £300 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Detached house | £250 to £350 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |

Checkatrade's damp survey guide gives higher figures of about £200 for a 2 bed flat, £400 for a 3 bed terrace and £600 for a 4 bed detached house, plus about £50 for a same day survey. Its prices were last updated in May 2024, so treat them as older guidance.

## What a good damp survey includes

- Where the damp is and how far it spreads
- Moisture readings, backed up by other evidence such as salts and the pattern of damp
- Outside checks: gutters, pointing, render, ground levels and air bricks
- Ventilation and heating, to rule condensation in or out
- A written report with photos and a clear cause

## Types of damp the survey should identify

- **Condensation**: moisture from inside the home settling on cold walls, often with black mould
- **Penetrating damp**: rain getting in through walls, roofs, gutters, windows or chimneys
- **Rising damp**: ground moisture rising up walls without a working damp proof course
- **Plumbing leaks**: hidden leaks from pipes, showers or heating

A damp surveyor will often also check timber for rot and woodworm, which is why lenders sometimes ask for a damp and timber survey.

## Free survey or independent survey?

Many damp proofing companies offer free surveys. The surveyor is often also selling the treatment, so a free survey can lead to a quote for chemical damp proofing whatever the cause. An independent damp surveyor charges a fee but has nothing to sell. For older Leeds stone terraces, where condensation and penetrating damp are common, a neutral opinion can save far more than it costs.
