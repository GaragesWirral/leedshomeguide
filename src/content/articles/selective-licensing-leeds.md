If you rent out a home in parts of East, South and West Leeds, you need a selective licence from Leeds City Council. Everything below comes from the council's own pages, read on 9 November 2026. Always check the council's map for your exact street.

## Which areas are covered?

Parts of six wards:

- Armley
- Beeston and Holbeck
- Burmantofts and Richmond Hill
- Hunslet and Riverside
- Gipton and Harehills
- Farnley and Wortley

The boundary does not follow whole wards, so use the interactive map and street list on the council's selective licensing page.

## What it costs

The total fee is £1,100 per property online or £1,225 on paper, paid in two parts. For applications made from 26 February 2026:

| | Online | Paper |
|---|---|---|
| First part (with your application) | £400 | £525 |
| Second part (if the licence is granted) | £700 | £700 |
| Second part for Leeds Rental Standard members | £550 | £550 |

## Who does not need one

The council lists these exemptions: holiday lets, business premises, socially let homes, student accommodation owned by a university, homes let to a family member (which has a strict legal meaning, so check first), properties where the landlord already holds an HMO licence, and empty properties. Some properties need an HMO licence instead.

## Before you apply

You must tell certain people in writing that you are applying: your mortgage lender, any other owners, anyone who manages the property, the proposed licence holder if that is not you, and some tenants or leaseholders. The council provides a template letter.

## What the licence asks of you

The conditions include, among others:

- An annual gas safety check by a Gas Safe registered engineer, with the certificate given to the council each year and to tenants
- Keeping the electrical installation and any appliances you provide safe, and giving the council a declaration if it asks
- A working smoke alarm on every storey with living accommodation
- A carbon monoxide alarm in any living room with a fixed combustion appliance (other than a gas cooker)
- Keeping any furniture you provide in a safe condition
