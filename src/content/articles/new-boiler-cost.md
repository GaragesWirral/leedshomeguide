A new boiler is one of the bigger bills most Leeds homeowners face, and quotes can vary by thousands of pounds. This guide sets out what a new boiler costs in 2026 using two national cost guides, what changes the price, and what to check before you sign a quote.

## How much does a new boiler cost?

There is no published price list for boilers in Leeds, so the figures below come from Checkatrade and MyBuilder. They are UK figures. Use them as a budget, then get at least two written quotes from Gas Safe registered engineers.

| Job | Typical UK cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Like for like combi boiler swap, supply and fitting | £2,500 to £4,000 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| System or conventional boiler to combi, supply and fitting | about £4,000 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| New boiler and installation, all types | £3,000 to £15,000 | Checkatrade, October 2026 |
| Boiler only (supply), most types | £500 to £3,500 | MyBuilder, October 2026 |
| Installation labour | £500 to £1,500 | MyBuilder, October 2026 |

The wide top end of the Checkatrade range covers oil, electric and biomass boilers. For a typical gas combi in a Leeds terrace or semi, the like for like figure is the most useful guide.

## New boiler prices by type

| Boiler type | Boiler only (MyBuilder) | Typical total fitted (Checkatrade) |
|---|---|---|
| Gas combi boiler | £600 to £2,000 | about £3,000 |
| System boiler | £500 to £2,500 | about £3,000 |
| Conventional (heat only) boiler | £600 to £3,500 | about £3,000 |
| Oil boiler | £1,000 to £3,500 | about £3,300 to £4,000 |
| Electric boiler | £600 to £2,500 | about £3,000 to £4,200 |

**Combi boilers** heat water on demand and need no tanks, so they suit most smaller Leeds homes such as back to backs, terraces and flats. **System boilers** use a hot water cylinder and suit homes with two bathrooms. **Conventional boilers** use a cylinder and a cold water tank in the loft, and are common in older houses.

## Should you repair or replace your boiler?

MyBuilder's guide suggests looking at the repair history. If repairs have passed about £500 in total, a new boiler often works out better through lower running costs and fewer breakdowns. Age matters too: parts for older boilers get harder to find. A Gas Safe engineer can tell you whether a repair is worth it.

## Grants for a new boiler

The government's **Boiler Upgrade Scheme** does not pay towards a new gas boiler. According to GOV.UK, it gives £7,500 towards an air source or ground source heat pump, £5,000 towards a biomass boiler and £2,500 towards an air to air heat pump, in England and Wales. Until March 2027 there is an extra £1,500 for heat pumps in homes heated by oil or LPG with no mains gas. Grant rules change, so check the [GOV.UK page](https://www.gov.uk/apply-boiler-upgrade-scheme/what-you-can-get) before you rely on it.

## Getting a new boiler fitted in Leeds

1. **Get two or three quotes.** Ask each engineer to visit, not quote over the phone, and to list the boiler model, warranty length, controls, filter and flush separately.
2. **Check Gas Safe.** Every engineer who works on gas must be on the [Gas Safe Register](https://www.gassaferegister.co.uk/). Check the licence number on their ID card.
3. **Ask about the warranty.** Many manufacturers give longer warranties when their own accredited installers fit the boiler and it is serviced every year.
4. **Ask for the paperwork.** You should get a Building Regulations compliance certificate for the new boiler, and the engineer should register it.
