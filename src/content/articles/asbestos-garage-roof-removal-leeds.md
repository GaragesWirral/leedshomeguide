Many Leeds garages built from the 1950s to the 1980s have corrugated asbestos cement roofs. Asbestos garage roofs in good condition are usually low risk, but they become a problem when they crack, get drilled or are broken up during removal. This guide covers the average cost of removal and disposal for garage roofs, what changes the price, and how disposal works in Leeds.

## How much does asbestos garage roof removal cost?

There are no published Leeds price lists for garage roofs, so this cost breakdown uses two national cost guides. Removal costs in the UK vary a lot by region and by job, so use them as a rough budget, then compare at least two written quotes.

| Job | Typical UK cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Single garage roof removal | £350 to £1,295 | MyBuilder, June 2026 |
| Double garage roof removal | £650 to £1,800 | MyBuilder, June 2026 |
| Whole garage removal (roof and sides) | £1,100 to £3,750 | MyBuilder, June 2026 |
| Asbestos roof removal | £950 to £3,750 (average £2,100) | Checkatrade, April 2026 |
| Asbestos testing or an asbestos survey | £50 to £245 for testing, £200 to £1,000 for a survey | MyBuilder; Checkatrade |

The asbestos garage roof replacement cost, for a new roof once the old one is off, is not included in these figures.

## Is my garage roof asbestos?

You cannot tell by looking. Corrugated grey sheets on older garage roofs are often asbestos cement, but the only way to know is to have a sample tested. Do not cut, sand or break a sheet to find out. If your garage roof contains asbestos, it is usually bonded asbestos, where the asbestos fibres are held in cement, which is why an asbestos cement roof is lower risk while it is intact. An asbestos survey or a single sample test will confirm it.

## Do I need a licensed contractor?

The Health and Safety Executive (HSE) decides which asbestos work needs a licence under the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012. Higher risk materials, such as sprayed coatings, lagging and most insulating board, need a contractor licensed by the HSE. Removing asbestos cement sheets from garage roofs is often non licensed work, so licensed removal is not always needed, but it still needs people who are trained and follow HSE guidance for safe removal. If you are not sure which applies to your roof, ask a surveyor or removal contractor and check the HSE guidance linked below.

## Replacing an asbestos garage roof

If you replace an asbestos garage roof, the new roof is usually fitted straight after the old sheets come off. Common replacement materials are box profile steel sheets, fibre cement sheets made without asbestos, or a flat roof such as felt or EPDM rubber laid on new boards. Ask for professional removal and the replacement to be priced separately, so you can compare the average cost of each line by line.

## Can I remove an asbestos garage roof myself?

The asbestos regulations mainly cover work done by businesses, and a householder can usually remove asbestos cement sheets from their own garage. If you choose DIY removal, keep the sheets whole, dampen them so fewer fibres are released, never use power tools, wear disposable overalls and a suitable mask, and double wrap every sheet before disposal. The Leeds Kirkstall site below only takes householder asbestos that is properly wrapped, and it takes no trade waste.

## Taking it to the Kirkstall site yourself

Leeds City Council runs a householder only asbestos disposal service at its Kirkstall waste transfer station on Evanston Avenue, LS4. From the council's page (read 9 November 2026):

- Booking only, with at least 48 hours notice. Email asbestos@leeds.gov.uk with your name, address, where the asbestos came from, your vehicle details, a contact number, the type and amount of material, and some preferred dates.
- Householders from outside Leeds cannot use it, and it does not accept asbestos from trade or businesses.
- Wear a high visibility jacket and protective footwear. Staff will not help you unload.
- Payment is by debit or credit card only.

| Weight | Charge |

Opening hours are Monday to Friday 8am to 4pm and Saturday 8am to midday.
