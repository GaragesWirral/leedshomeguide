An asbestos survey tells you where asbestos is in a building and what condition it is in. This guide uses two national cost guides to show what each type of survey costs in 2026, and how to choose the right one.

## How much does an asbestos survey cost?

| Survey | Typical UK cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Management survey, 2 to 3 bedroom house | £150 to £350 (average £250) | Checkatrade, May 2026 |
| Management survey, shop with flat above | £250 to £400 (average £325) | Checkatrade, May 2026 |
| Management survey, commercial property | £200 to £600 (average £400) | Checkatrade, May 2026 |
| Refurbishment or demolition survey | £300 to £400 (average £350) | Checkatrade, May 2026 |
| Residential management survey | £165 to £385 | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Residential refurbishment and demolition survey | £300 to £500+ | MyBuilder, August 2026 |
| Commercial refurbishment and demolition survey | £500 to £1,000+ | MyBuilder, August 2026 |

There is no published Leeds price list, so treat these as UK budgets and get two quotes.

## Asbestos testing costs

| Test | Typical cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard sample | about £50 per sample | MyBuilder |
| Comprehensive lab analysis | £100 to £250 | MyBuilder |
| DIY testing kit | £35 to £50 per kit | MyBuilder |

A DIY kit means you take the sample yourself, which can release fibres. A trained surveyor will wet the material, seal the sample and seal the sample point.

## Which asbestos survey do you need?

- **Management survey**: the standard survey for a building in normal use. It finds asbestos that could be disturbed day to day and assesses its condition.
- **Refurbishment and demolition survey**: needed before building work. It is intrusive and covers only the area to be worked on, which may need to be empty.
- **Sample test**: for a single material, such as an asbestos garage roof or an Artex ceiling.

## What changes the price?

- **Size of the building** and the number of rooms to inspect
- **Number of samples** sent to the lab
- **Survey type**, as intrusive surveys take longer
- **Access**, such as lofts, voids and crawl spaces
- **Turnaround**, as faster lab results can cost more

## After the survey

If asbestos is found, it does not always need removing. Material in good condition can often be left and recorded, or sealed.
