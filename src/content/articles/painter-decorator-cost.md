Painting is mostly labour, so the price depends on how long the job takes. This guide sets out day rates, room prices and whole house prices for 2026, using two national cost guides.

## Painter and decorator day rates

| Rate | Typical UK cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Day rate | £250 to £400, average £325 plus paint | Checkatrade, June 2026 |
| Hourly rate | about £32 | Checkatrade, June 2026 |
| Day rate | £150 to £250 | MyBuilder |
| Hourly rate | £20 to £40 | MyBuilder |

The two guides are some way apart. Checkatrade's average sits above MyBuilder's whole range, so it is worth getting at least two quotes and comparing what each includes.

Leeds weather means exterior work is usually booked from late spring to early autumn, when walls are dry. Scaffolding for upper floors is often extra.

## What pushes the price up

- **Preparation.** Cracks, flaking paint and old wallpaper take time to put right.
- **Premium paint.** Designer brands cost more than standard trade paint.
- **Ceilings and woodwork.** High ceilings, coving, skirting and spindles add hours.
- **Empty or full rooms.** Moving and covering furniture takes time.
- **Colour changes.** Dark to light usually needs extra coats.
