Asbestos removal prices depend on the type of material, how much there is, whether the work needs an HSE licence and how the waste is disposed of. This guide uses two national cost guides to show typical 2026 prices.

|---|---|---|
| Most asbestos removal jobs | £950 to £3,750 (typical total about £1,750) | Checkatrade, April 2026 |

## Surveys and testing

Most removal firms want to know what they are dealing with before they quote. Checkatrade puts an asbestos survey at £200 to £1,000 depending on size and scope, and MyBuilder puts testing at £50 to £245. See our [asbestos survey cost guide](/guides/asbestos-survey-cost/).

## Licensed or non licensed work?

Some asbestos work, such as removing sprayed coatings or lagging, must be done by an HSE licensed contractor. Much of the asbestos in homes, such as asbestos cement garage roofs, is usually non licensed work but must still be done carefully. HSE says breaking or smashing asbestos cement sheets releases far more fibres, so sheets should come off whole and be wrapped. Licensed work costs more because of enclosures, air monitoring and notification.

## What changes the price?

- **Type of asbestos and material**: lagging and insulation board cost more than cement sheets
- **Amount and access**: a whole ceiling costs more than a small panel
- **Licensed or not**
- **Disposal**: asbestos must go to a licensed site
- **Making good**: replastering or a new roof is extra

## Remove, seal or leave?

Asbestos in good condition that will not be disturbed can often be left and recorded, or sealed. Removal makes sense when it is damaged or in the way of building work.
