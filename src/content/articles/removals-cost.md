Removal prices depend on how much you own, how far you are moving and how much help you want with packing. This guide uses two national cost guides to show what moving house costs in 2026.

## How much do removals cost?

| House size | Removal | Packing | Average total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 bedroom | £450 | £250 | £750 |
| 2 bedroom | £600 | £325 | £925 |
| 3 bedroom | £900 | £400 | £1,300 |
| 4 bedroom | £1,200 | £600 | £1,800 |

Source: Checkatrade, April 2026.

MyBuilder (August 2026) gives a wider range, from about £409 for a 1 bedroom flat to about £3,147 for a 5 bedroom house, and £1,200 to £1,700 for a full service 3 bedroom move.

## Keeping removal costs down

1. **Declutter first.** Every box costs money to move. See [rubbish removal in Leeds](/trades/rubbish-removal/).
2. **Avoid peak days.** Fridays and the end of the month are busiest.
3. **Pack yourself** and pay only for fragile items to be packed.
4. **Get a survey.** A written fixed price after a home visit avoids surprises on the day.
5. **Check parking.** A long carry from the lorry adds time on Leeds terraced streets.
