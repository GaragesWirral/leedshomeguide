A handyman is usually the cheapest way to get a list of small jobs done in one visit. Most charge by the hour or the day, often with a minimum charge. This guide sets out what handymen charge in 2026, using two national cost guides.

## How much does a handyman cost per hour?

| Area | Typical hourly rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| UK average | about £30 | Checkatrade and MyBuilder, 2026 |
| London | about £50 | Checkatrade and MyBuilder, 2026 |
| South East | about £35 | MyBuilder, October 2026 |
| North West | about £25 | MyBuilder, October 2026 |
| Wales | about £20 | MyBuilder, October 2026 |

MyBuilder says a self employed handyman typically charges £20 to £30 an hour, with a full range of £15 to £60 depending on experience and location. Neither guide gives a Leeds figure, but rates in the north sit at or below the national average. Checkatrade notes that larger companies that subcontract work often charge £60 an hour or more.

## Minimum charges and day rates

- **Minimum call out:** about £40 to £50 (Checkatrade), or £40 to £60 covering the first hour (MyBuilder)
- **Average visit:** about £80 for a small job (Checkatrade)
- **Day rate:** about £175 to £200 for a 7 to 8 hour day at £25 an hour (MyBuilder); about £350 a day in London (Checkatrade)
- **Travel:** MyBuilder notes travel charges of £20 to £50 if the handyman comes a long way

## How to keep the cost down

- **Group jobs.** One half day visit is far cheaper than several separate call outs.
- **Send a list with photos.** The handyman can quote, bring the right tools and avoid a second trip.
- **Buy the parts.** If you have chosen fittings, buy them yourself, or agree that the handyman supplies them at cost.
- **Ask for a fixed price** on bigger single jobs such as a set of shelves.

## Handyman or tradesperson?

A handyman or handyperson is best for small jobs and a mix of small tasks in one visit, such as furniture assembly, shelves, sealant and minor repairs. For a single large job, a specialist tradesperson may be quicker and is sometimes required. The going rate outside London is usually close to the national average of about £30 an hour, but always ask whether there's a minimum charge or a call out fee before booking.

## What a handyman should not do

Gas work must be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and new circuits or a consumer unit should go to a registered electrician who can certify the work. See [plumbers in Leeds](/trades/plumbers/) and [electricians in Leeds](/trades/electricians/).
