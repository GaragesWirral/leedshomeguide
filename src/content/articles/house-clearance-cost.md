House clearance is usually priced by how much there is to take, measured in van loads, plus extra for items that cost more to dispose of. This guide sets out what clearance costs in 2026, from two national cost guides, and when the council or a skip may be cheaper.

## How much does house clearance cost?

Checkatrade prices clearance by van load:

| Load | Weight | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| Extra small, about a quarter van | up to 250kg | from £175 |
| Small, about half a van | up to 500kg | from £325 |
| Medium, about three quarters of a van | up to 750kg | from £400 |
| Large, a full van | up to 1,000kg | from £595 |

Source: Checkatrade, June 2026.

## Cheaper options in Leeds

- **Unwanted items collection.** Leeds City Council's [unwanted items collection](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/bins-and-recycling/book-an-unwanted-items-collection) takes up to 5 bulky items, with one free collection a year and further collections at £30.
- **Household waste recycling centres.** If you can take items yourself, Leeds sites accept furniture, fridges and mattresses.
- **Charities.** Some furniture charities collect good quality items free.
- **A skip.** Checkatrade gives £125 to £320 for skip hire if you are happy to load it. See [skip hire in Leeds](/trades/skip-hire/).

## Probate and full house clearances

A full house clearance after a death, or for probate, is often the biggest job. A good house clearance company will sort items to sell, donate or recycle, set aside anything valuable or personal, and give you a written quote. Some house clearance companies offer a lower price if they can sell some of the contents. Labour, the number of van loads and how many items need special disposal are the main parts of the overall cost, and VAT may be added if the firm is VAT registered.

## Garage clearance cost

A garage clearance is usually charged by volume like any other clearance. MyBuilder gives £100 to £250 for a garage or shed. If the garage itself needs to come down, see our [garage demolition cost guide](/guides/garage-demolition-cost/).

## Check the waste carrier licence

If your waste is fly tipped, you can be held responsible even if you paid someone to take it. Anyone who carries waste for money must be registered with the Environment Agency, so ask for the registration number and check it before they load anything.
