Pest control is usually priced per treatment or per visit, not by the hour. This guide uses Checkatrade's 2026 price guide and Leeds City Council's own pest control information.

## How much does pest control cost?

| Pest or treatment | Typical UK cost | Average |
|---|---|---|
| Rats or mice (two visits) | £120 to £240 | £180 |
| Wasp nest (one nest) | £75 to £200 | £137.50 |
| Fleas (per property) | £80 to £220 | £150 |
| Bed bugs (per property) | £100 to £280 | £190 |
| Bed bug heat and insecticide (up to 3 bedrooms) | £1,000 to £1,500 | £1,200 |
| Cockroaches (two visits) | £120 to £260 | £190 |
| Ants (per treatment) | £100 to £230 | £165 |
| Squirrels (two visits) | £100 to £300 | £200 |
| Moths (per treatment) | £150 to £2,000 | £500 |
| Pest control specialist, per day | £200 to £260 | £230 |

Source: Checkatrade, April 2026.

## Leeds City Council pest control

Leeds City Council runs its own pest control service for residents and businesses. According to the council's page:

- its officers are BASIS Prompt registered and members of the National Pest Technicians Association
- for most pests, the price includes all the visits needed to clear the current problem, except bedbugs and wasps
- you request treatment online and should allow up to 5 working days to be contacted
- businesses can get a free survey and quote

The council publishes its charges on a separate page for each pest, so check the [council's pest control page](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/environmental-health/pest-control) for current prices.

## What changes the price?

- **Type of pest** and how established it is
- **Number of visits** needed
- **Size of the property**
- **Proofing**: sealing entry points costs extra but stops pests coming back
- **Urgency**: same day call outs can cost more

## Council or private firm?

The council's all visits price can be good value for rats and mice. A private firm is often faster, which matters for wasps near a door or rodents indoors. See [pest control in Leeds](/trades/pest-control/) and [wasp nest removal in Leeds](/trades/pest-control/wasp-nest-removal/).
